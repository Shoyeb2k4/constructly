# User Guide

_Constructly for construction company owners and project managers. India-first: ₹, GST, and site-ready workflows._

## Roles

- **Owner** — full access: creates projects and vendors, approves purchase orders, records and deletes payments, manages settings.
- **Project Manager** — works on assigned projects: records attendance, creates POs and payments, but cannot approve/delete financial records or manage vendors.

## Dashboard

Your company at a glance, understandable in ten seconds:
- **KPIs** — active projects, upcoming deadlines, overdue, today's labour, budget used, pending approvals. Each is clickable.
- **Spend trend** + **cost split** (labour vs procurement).
- **Procurement command center** — spend, GST paid, outstanding + pending payments, top vendors, spend by project, top materials, upcoming deliveries, monthly trend.
- **Attention center** — the day's action list: approvals, overdue projects, budget warnings, deadlines, stale projects.
- **Activity feed** — recent changes across every module, deep-linked.

## Projects

Create a project with a code, client, site, overall budget, and an optional **dedicated procurement budget**. Inside a project:
- **Overview** — budget card showing overall budget (used/remaining) and the procurement budget (budget/used/remaining) distinctly, plus client & site details.
- **Procurement** — the purchasing center: budget/spent/remaining/POs/vendors/outstanding cards, vendors-involved table (click a vendor to see only their transactions here), scoped POs and payments.
- **Labour** — man-days, estimated cost, and recent attendance for this project.
- **Deadlines · Documents · Notes · Activity.**

Track projects as a virtualized table or a Kanban board (drag between lifecycle stages).

## Labour & Attendance

Built for sub-one-minute site entry, and it works **offline**:
- Organize labour as **Category → Subcategory** (e.g. Carpenter → Main Carpenter / Carpenter Helper). No individual worker tracking.
- Pick a project (it remembers the last) and date (defaults today); tap the steppers to set the headcount per subcategory. **Copy yesterday** fills a repeat day instantly.
- Costs use a **rate snapshot** frozen at entry — changing a rate next month never alters past records.
- Export the daily sheet or a date range to PDF/Excel.
- Entries save even with no signal and sync automatically when you reconnect — you never lose data.

## Vendors

A vendor supplies many projects. Each vendor profile is a growing ledger:
- **Header** — GSTIN, PAN, contact, terms; four stat cards (total purchase, outstanding, projects served, POs).
- **Tabs** — Overview, Projects (click to filter the whole profile to one project), Purchase Orders, Payments, Documents, and **Outstanding** with aging buckets (0–30 / 31–60 / 60+ days).

## Purchase Orders

- Header (project, vendor, dates, expected delivery) + line items with **material, category, quantity, unit, rate, and GST%** (presets 0/5/12/18/28 or free entry). The footer shows Subtotal / GST / Grand Total live, plus budget impact.
- **Approval** (if enabled in Settings): Draft → Submitted → Approved. Approved POs are **locked**; use **Revise** to make a linked new draft. **Duplicate** clones any PO to a fresh draft for repeat purchases.

## Payments

Record a payment against a vendor and project, optionally linked to a specific PO (leave it blank for advances or on-account payments). Choose the mode (bank transfer, UPI, cash, cheque, card), add a reference (one-click copy), and the **outstanding updates everywhere automatically**. A soft warning appears if a payment exceeds the outstanding — allowed, since advances are normal.

## Reports

Generate Project, Labour, Procurement, Vendor, Budget-vs-Actual, and Deadline reports with date/project/vendor filters. Every number matches the dashboard and project views exactly (same shared calculations). Export any report to PDF or Excel.

## Settings

- Toggle **PO approval** on/off.
- Manage **material category** presets (Cement, Steel, Sand & Aggregate, Electrical, Plumbing, Paint, Hardware, Other) used across POs, filters, and analytics.
- Switch light/dark theme.

## Tips

- ⌘K opens the command palette to jump anywhere.
- The app is fully keyboard-navigable and works on your phone on-site.
